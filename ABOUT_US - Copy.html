A brief history

Zenith JDM Imports was founded in Johannesburg as a specialist importer and dealer of Japanese Domestic Market (JDM) vehicles. What began as a small operation sourcing individual cars for local enthusiasts has grown into a dedicated dealership that manages the full import pipeline from Japanese auction sourcing through to shipping, compliance, and after-sales support. The business has built its reputation on transparency, documented vehicle history, and deep product knowledge of JDM performance models.

Some Aesthetic cars to catch your eye.

WHO ARE WE

We are specialist importer and dealers of Japanese Domestic Market (JDM) vehicles, bringing some of the most sought after cars from Japan directly to enthusiasts and collectors. Our focus is on sourcing high-quality, low-mileage vehicles straight from Japanese auctions and dealerships, cars that were originally built for the Japanese market and never officially sold elsewhere. This means our customers get access to unique models, trims and specifications that simply aren't available through standard dealerships.

WHAT WE SELL

Our inventory typically includes iconic performance cars such as the Nissan Skyline GT-R, Toyota Supra, Mazda RX-7, Honda NSX, Subaru Impreza WRX STI and Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution, along with rarer JDM-only models like Kei cars, drift-spec vehicles and limited production variants. Every car we bring in has a documented history, including auction sheets that detail mileage, condition grade, accident history and modifications, so buyers know exactly what they're getting before it arrives.

OUR PROCESS

We handle the entire import process from start to finish, sourcing the vehicle at auction in Japan, arranging shipping, managing customs clearance and ensuring the car meets all necessary compliance and requirements for the destination country. Because import regulations can be complex, we guide clients through eligibility, paperwork and timelines so there are no surprises along the way.

WHY CHOOSE US

Beyond the cars themselves, we pride ourselves on transparency and expertise. Our team has deep knowledge of JDM culture, model history and mechanical specifics, so we can help clients choose a vehicle that fits their goals, whether that's a weekend track car, a daily driver with character or a long term collector's investment. We also offer post sale support, including help finding OEM parts, specialists for maintenance and guidance on modifications, so the relationship doesn't end once the car is delivered.
